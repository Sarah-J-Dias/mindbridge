Share Buttons Breadcrumbs Your browser is not supported Switch to Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari Main page content Title Mental Health Intro Mental health is an essential part of overall health and well-being, influencing how people think, feel, act, and relate to others. While everyone experiences challenges that can affect their mental health, some individuals may develop mental illnesses that can impact mood, thinking, behavior, and daily functioning. About 22% of U.S. adults are affected by mental illness, including depression and anxiety. More than 5% of adults experience serious mental illness (SMI), a level of mental illness that substantially interferes with major life activities, including bipolar disorder, major depressive disorder, and schizophrenia. If left untreated, these can have a detrimental impact on individuals and their families. SAMHSA leads federal efforts to promote treatment and recovery of mental illness and SMI to improve the health and wellbeing of individuals, families, and communities, so that all people in the U.S. are able live fulfilling, independent and productive lives.
